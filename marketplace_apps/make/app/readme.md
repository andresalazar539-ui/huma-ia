# HUMA IA

HUMA IA is an AI sales assistant for WhatsApp, Instagram and website chat. It answers leads, qualifies them, books appointments and closes sales. This app connects your HUMA IA account to Make.

## Connect

1. Add any HUMA IA module to a scenario and click **Create a connection**.
2. Log in to HUMA IA (if needed) and click **Conectar**.
3. Done. You can disconnect at any time in HUMA IA: Integrations > AI assistants.

Only the account owner or an admin can use the triggers (they receive data about every lead of the account). Team members can use the action modules on the conversations they can see.

## Triggers (instant)

- **Watch New Leads**: a lead talks to HUMA IA for the first time.
- **Watch Qualified Leads**: HUMA IA qualified a lead and handed it to your team.
- **Watch Confirmed Appointments**: HUMA IA confirmed an appointment in your calendar.
- **Watch Approved Payments**: a payment made inside a conversation was approved.
- **Watch Signed Contracts** and **Watch Refused Contracts**: the lead signed or refused a contract sent by HUMA IA.

## Actions and searches

- **Send a Message**: sends a text to an existing conversation, on the lead channel.
- **Move Lead to Stage**: moves the lead in the sales funnel.
- **Add a Note**: adds a note that HUMA IA reads when answering the lead.
- **Hand Off Conversation**: a person takes over (HUMA IA stays silent) or gives it back to HUMA IA.
- **Get a Conversation**: lead data, notes, appointment and the last 20 messages.
- **Search Conversations**: find conversations by lead name or phone.
- **Make an API Call**: any call to the HUMA IA API.

HUMA IA never starts a conversation with a new contact from Make: messages go only to conversations that already exist.
